Free guide

Agent-ready in 10 steps.

What a store can do today so agents find it, understand it and buy there. Most steps also pay off right now, when ChatGPT or Gemini recommend stores.

  1. 01

    Show the total price up front.

    Price including shipping on the product page. Put discounts in the price, not at checkout.

    Agents calculate the full cost, including shipping, returns and warranty.
  2. 02

    Returns and shipping as text.

    Return window, free returns and delivery time on the product page, not just in the terms.

    86 % chose the better return policy at equal prices.
  3. 03

    Let people buy without an account.

    Offer guest checkout. A login wall ends almost every agent purchase.

    81 % reached the end of checkout with guest checkout, 0 % with a required login.
  4. 04

    Facts as text, not images.

    Sizes, dimensions, materials and what's in the box as readable text. Agents can't see a size chart image.

    An agent needs to establish what a product does, what it costs and what limits apply.
  5. 05

    Write for specific use cases.

    Say who your product is best for, and for what. For broad requests, the cheapest product usually wins.

    Dyson vs. Shark: 9 % Dyson for the broad ask, 88 % for daily use.
  6. 06

    Show your reviews.

    Number of reviews and average rating, clearly visible on the product page.

    Three of four agents tested cited reviews in 30 to 41 % of their picks.
  7. 07

    Trust beats discounts.

    Warranty, authorised dealer, trust badges. Small discounts do nothing, huge ones look suspicious.

    Discounts under 10 % changed nothing. Above 50 %, agents steered clear.
  8. 08

    Be where agents search.

    Google Shopping, the big marketplaces and the price comparison sites in your category. Agents often start in catalogs.

    Nearly a third of agent spend at the top 50 merchants went to marketplaces and aggregators.
  9. 09

    Check your bot blocker.

    Cloudflare and other protection services can turn AI agents away. You never notice.

    On Amazon, 3 of 4 agents were blocked in every attempt.
  10. 10

    Keep data and feeds current.

    Structured data (schema.org) for product, price, availability, shipping and returns. Price and stock correct in every feed.

    Agents use similar sources: 73 % of one agent's picks also showed up with another.

Figures: a16z, "How to Win Bots and Influence Purchases", 23 Nov 2026.
